Studio

Twelve people, one long table, a site visit most Fridays.

What we believe

A plan is a promise about how someone will move through a room before the room exists. We spend most of our time testing that promise — walking the empty site with tape on the floor, mocking up a stair in the workshop before it's poured, asking the client to stand where the sink will be. Software renders convince clients; they rarely convince us.

We keep the material list short on purpose. Concrete, timber, block, glass — used honestly enough that you can tell what's structural and what's decoration, because on our jobs there usually isn't a difference.

The best compliment a finished building gets is that no one asks who designed it.
