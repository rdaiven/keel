How it works

There is no setup wizard and no import consultant. Most crews send their first real invoice the day they sign up.

  1. Log the trip. Who, where, what was agreed. Twenty seconds, usually while the clients are still finding their car keys.
  2. Send the invoice. Built from the trip, sent by link or email. The client can pay from their phone before they've left the marina.
  3. Record what moved. Fuel, bait, mooring fees, crew shares — photographed or typed, filed against the trip automatically.
  4. Hand over a clean ledger. At tax time, export the year and give it to your accountant. That conversation gets much shorter.

Fair questions

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You export it and keep it — CSV and PDF, every plan, including free. Your account stays readable for ninety days after you cancel, then we delete it. We'd rather you stay because the tool is good, not because leaving is hard.

Does it really work without signal?

Yes. Invoices, trips, and expenses are all created on the device and sync when you're back in range. Sending an invoice needs a connection — everything before that moment doesn't.

We're not a boat. Can we still use it?

Plenty of crews aren't — mobile mechanics, surveyors, small film crews. If your work happens in trips or jobs rather than at a desk, the shape fits. The nautical words are ours; the ledger doesn't mind.

Is there a contract or a setup fee?

Neither. Month to month, cancel in two clicks, and the free plan has no time limit. If a month goes badly, downgrade for the off-season and come back when the bookings do.

Who do I talk to when something breaks?

A person, by email — hello@example.com. We answer within one working day, usually faster during the season, because that's when it matters.

Keep the ledger. Lose the shoebox.

Start on the free plan with a real trip and a real invoice. If it doesn't earn its keep by the end of the month, export your data and go — no hard feelings.
