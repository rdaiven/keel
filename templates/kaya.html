How it works

No branches, no paperwork. Most people send their first payment inside a minute of downloading Kaya.

  1. Link an account. Connect a debit card or bank account — Kaya never holds your money longer than a transfer takes.
  2. Add the people you split with. Roommates, travel friends, family — a group takes ten seconds to set up.
  3. Send, request, settle. Every payment shows up for everyone in the group, so nobody has to ask "did that go through?"

Held to a bank's standard

Kaya doesn't lend, invest, or hold your money longer than a transfer requires — a narrower job, done more carefully.
