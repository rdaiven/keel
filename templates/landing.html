Sewn at the foot of the ridge

Ridgeline started as one repaired pack — ours. The seam held for nine hundred miles, friends asked for the same fix, and the fix became a workshop. We still machine every buckle blank and cut every panel in the same two rooms.

We make three products. When something wears out, send it back: we repair it free, forever, and the trail miles stay yours.

The pack outlasted the boots, the tent, and the truck.

— Mara T., Ridgeline 28 no. 0041
