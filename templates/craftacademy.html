The curriculum

Six modules, released weekly. Everything stays unlocked once it's yours — go back anytime.

Module 1 — Grip, posture, and your first strokes
  • Choosing a brush pen (and why the cheap one is fine to start)
  • Upstrokes vs. downstrokes: the one rule that fixes 80% of mistakes
  • Warm-up drill sheet (printable, 10 minutes a day)
Module 2 — Lowercase letterforms
  • The four letter families and how to drill them together
  • Connecting letters without losing consistent slant
  • Common mistakes gallery: real student pages, annotated
Module 3 — Uppercase & flourishing
  • Capital letters that don't fight your lowercase rhythm
  • Adding flourishes without overdoing it
  • Live-drawn demo: an entire quote, start to finish
Module 4 — Composition & layout
  • Laying out a quote before you touch ink
  • Hierarchy: making one line matter more than the rest
  • Working from a thumbnail sketch to a final piece
Module 5 — Color & materials
  • Watercolor backgrounds that won't fight your ink
  • Paper stocks worth the extra cost, and ones that aren't
  • Building a starter kit for under ₱2,000
Module 6 — Your final piece & critique
  • Planning a piece worth framing
  • Submitting for live critique with the instructor
  • Graduation showcase — your work, shared with the cohort
