Sections · Calls to action

Seven ways to ask, built from keel's layout and components — one ask per band, never two. Every section below is rendered as a real full-bleed band, with its exact markup underneath. Copy the markup, keep your tokens — this page follows whatever design system you generate on the design system page.

Copy inside the sections is placeholder — a fictional studio called Harbor & Pine — so what you paste reads like a page, not like a template.

Accent band

The main ask on the accent background. The ghost button borrows currentColor so it works on any accent.

Two project slots open this autumn

We book a season ahead. If the timing works for you, the best move is to say hello early.

Start the conversation
Markup — accent band 
<section class="k-section k-section--accent">
  <div class="k-container--narrow k-container k-center">
    <h2>Two project slots open this autumn</h2>
    <p>We book a season ahead. If the timing works for you, the best
    move is to say hello early.</p>
    <a class="k-btn k-btn--ghost" href="#"
       style="--k-btn-fg: currentColor; --k-btn-border: currentColor; --k-btn-bg-hover: var(--k-accent-strong)">
      Start the conversation</a>
  </div>
</section>

Demo request

The pitch and the form side by side. The form has no action — wire it to your backend.

See Ledger on your own numbers

Half an hour, your last three months imported live, and every pricing question answered plainly.

  • No slides — just the product
  • A trial that stays yours afterwards
  • We follow up once, then stop

Only used to arrange the demo.
