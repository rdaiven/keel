Five ways to put a number on the page and stand behind it. Same rules as the main sections page: every pattern below is rendered as a real full-bleed band, with its exact markup underneath, and it re-renders in whatever tokens the design system page gives you.

Prices inside the sections are placeholder — fictional harbor-town businesses again — and each pattern keeps to one currency, so what you paste stays coherent.

Pricing FAQ

The two answers everyone needs as cards, then an accordion for the rest. Native details — no JavaScript.

Money questions

Deposits

Half up front, half at handover. The deposit is what holds your slot in the season.

Refunds

Walk away before the draft and the deposit comes back, minus the first week.

Why is there no hourly rate?

Hourly billing rewards slow work. A fixed price agreed up front means the risk of a long week is ours, not yours.

What happens if the project grows?

We stop and re-quote before doing the extra work. Nothing new is built on a number you have not seen.

Can I pay in parts?

Yes — thirds is common: at start, at draft, at handover. Say so before we begin and it goes in the agreement.
