Sections · People & contact

The sections about the humans behind a site — teams, profiles, open roles, and the ways to reach them. Like the main sections page, every pattern below is rendered as a real full-bleed band, with its exact markup underneath.

Copy inside the sections is placeholder — the same fictional studio, Harbor & Pine — so what you paste reads like a page, not like a template.
