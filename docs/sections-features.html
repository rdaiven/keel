What the studio does

Three services, no add-ons. Each one ends with something you own outright.

01

Identity

A name treatment, a palette, and the rules for using them — short enough to remember, firm enough to hold.

02

Websites

Static sites in plain HTML and CSS. Fast because there is nothing to slow them down.

03

Care

A yearly check: links, dates, hosting, one round of edits. Most clients need nothing else.

Icon feature grid

Six compact features, each led by a built-in icon in an accent-soft rounded square.

Six small promises

Kept the same way on every project, whether it is one page or eight.

Walked before launch

Every page proofed and every link clicked before anyone else sees the site.

A reply, not a ticket

Questions go to a person with a name, and come back within two working days.

The yearly check

Links, dates, and hosting looked over once a year, whether you remember to ask or not.

Findable pages

Titles that say what the page is, so search engines and people both understand it.

Yours on paper

Domains, hosting, and logins registered in your name from the first day.

Built to be kept

Plain files that will still open, and still make sense, years after handover.

Bento grid

An asymmetric card arrangement: one full-width tinted card, then supporting pairs. Classes only.

The work, arranged by weight

One big thing, then the pairs that support it. No card here is filler.

The main thing

A site you can hand to anyone

Plain HTML and CSS, a one-page guide, and a recorded walkthrough. If we disappeared tomorrow, your site would not notice.

Fast

Nothing to wait for

No frameworks, no font downloads, no consent banners. Pages arrive before the doubt does.

Quiet

Zero third parties

No analytics, no embeds, no requests leaving the page. What you read here stays here.

Owned

Everything in your name

Domain, hosting, and logins registered to you from day one — not parked with the studio.

Kept

A yearly once-over

Links, dates, and hosting checked every year, with one round of edits included.
