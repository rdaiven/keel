Sections · Headers & footers

Page chrome as copyable sections — navbars, a glass header, and footers from simple to mega. Each one renders here as a demo band in the middle of the page; the page's own real header and footer stay where they belong.

Copy inside the sections is placeholder — the same fictional studio, Harbor & Pine — so what you paste reads like a page, not like a template.
